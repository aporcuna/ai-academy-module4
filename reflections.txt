
Although I began this project without deep expertise in Python, my technical foundation and the resources provided by the AI Academy made the learning curve manageable. 
The primary challenge involved the extraction of high-quality data from diverse formats, specifically PDF documents and video files. 
By implementing Whisper for transcription and PyMuPDF for document parsing, I found the extraction phase to be surprisingly efficient

The development process was facilitated by a custom Gemini GEM, allowing for an iterative, step-by-step construction of the RAG pipeline. 
This modular approach provided a clear conceptual understanding of the retrieval-augmented generation lifecycle. 
For the implementation, I prioritized a local environment using Ollama (Llama 3) and Hugging Face embeddings. 
Despite the performance constraints of local inference on a laptop (specifically an Intel Ultra 7 155U with 32GB of RAM) this choice offered a robust understanding of the trade-offs between local privacy and processing speed

Finally, I must say that the code is a POC like solution, with some minimal structure.
